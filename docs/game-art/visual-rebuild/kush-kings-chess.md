# Kush Kings Chess — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/kush-kings-chess/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc-chess-git`
Rebuild class: new visual client over chess rules/networking where reliable.

## Visual goal

A premium cannabis-fantasy chess room with original piece silhouettes and a board readable enough for serious play.

## Required assets

- logo/key art.
- six unique piece archetypes × two sides.
- board/material system and square coordinates.
- selected, legal move, capture, last-move, check and checkmate indicators.
- captured-piece tray.
- player banners/clocks/status as applicable.
- lobby, room code, chat/reconnect, victory/draw/resign screens.
- optional subtle room/environment framing.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: board uses nearly full width; player banners above/below; captured pieces compact; chat in drawer. Tablet: board + side status/chat in landscape. Desktop: centered board with narrow side panels only.

Twelve piece designs, board, state-overlay strip, phone/tablet/desktop match frames.
