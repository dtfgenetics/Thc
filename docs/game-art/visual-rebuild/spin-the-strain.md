# Spin the Strain — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/spin-the-strain/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new physical-wheel presentation over deterministic selection logic.

## Visual goal

A satisfying showpiece spinner with three visually distinct wheel modes, tactile pointer behavior and celebratory result cards.

## Required assets

- logo/key art.
- three illustrated wheel skins.
- segment treatment system for the 18-entry cycles/modes.
- physical pointer, center cap/logo and spin-button family.
- spin blur/highlight, tick, stop/reveal and celebration effects.
- result cards, history tokens and mode selector.
- sound-toggle/feedback symbols.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: wheel fills width, controls/result below; no side panels. Tablet: wheel plus result/history pane in landscape. Desktop: large centered wheel with compact history/mode rail.

## First approval pack

Three wheel concepts, pointer/center system, one result reveal, phone/tablet/desktop layouts.
