# Strain Showdown — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/strain-showdown/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: premium TCG presentation over reusable engine/data.

## Visual goal

A collectible battle-card game with eight instantly recognizable families: Kush, Haze, Skunk, Gas, Cookies, Fruit, Purple and Frost. Cards must feel collectible at thumbnail size and readable during battle.

## Required assets

- logo, card back and battle-board master.
- 96 strain illustrations/treatments tied to the approved roster.
- eight family frame/emblem systems.
- Base/Select/Elite tier treatments.
- Vigor, Power, stage/summon and status icons.
- 32 location-card illustrations/treatments where current design calls for four per family.
- deck, hand, grave/KO, lane and focus-token graphics.
- summon, attack, damage, KO, victory and pack/opening effects.

## Responsive composition

Phone landscape preferred for battle: hand scroll/fan at bottom, three lanes centered, opponent state top, enlarged card on tap. Tablet: visible hand plus board with detail inspector. Desktop: full three-lane board, hover/tap card zoom and peripheral deck/status areas.

## First approval pack

One family style sheet, eight emblem concepts, six representative cards across tiers, battle board, phone/tablet/desktop battle frames.
