# PhenoQuest: The Living Seed Vault — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/phenoquest/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Catching-phenos`
Rebuild class: new RPG world/character renderer over useful genetics/lineage/progression systems.

## Visual goal

A colorful genetics adventure through Verdantia where cuttings, phenotypes, keeper traits and lineage restoration become visible world mechanics rather than forms.

## Required assets

- logo/key art and world map.
- player character, NPCs and phenotype-creature cast.
- walking/idle/interact/battle animations.
- region tile sets, battle backgrounds, labs, seed vaults, clone/rooting spaces and environmental props.
- cuttings, clones, seeds, inventory/item icons.
- dialogue portraits and quest markers.
- phenotype comparison, trait, crossing and lineage-tree UI.
- battle/action VFX, keeper reveal and progression unlocks.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait/top-down: playfield top/center with contextual action bar bottom; inventory/journal/lineage open as full sheets. Tablet: world plus narrow quest/inventory rail. Desktop: larger world viewport with compact DOM HUD and optional side journal.

## First approval pack

Player + four representative creatures/NPCs, one Verdantia region, battle frame, phenotype compare screen and phone/tablet/desktop world layouts.
