# Who Took It? — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/who-took-it/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc-guess-who`
Rebuild class: new case-board renderer/UI over useful mystery/question logic.

## Visual goal

A playful late-night grow-room mystery: collectible illustrated suspect portraits organized on an evidence board. Original case-file language, not imitation of another deduction-board game's look.

## Required assets

- logo/key art.
- 25 final suspect portraits with strongly different silhouettes/features.
- five missing-item illustrations.
- evidence-room/case-board master.
- suspect normal/hover/selected/eliminated/accused/culprit states.
- item-card states.
- question card, YES/NO treatment, clue pins/string/tape and evidence tokens.
- interrogation, accusation, reveal, victory and failure scenes.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: suspects become a responsive 5-column compact board with tap-to-enlarge inspector; questions/actions live in bottom sheet. Tablet: 5×5 board plus side question pane. Desktop: full case board with evidence controls to one side. Portrait art must remain legible at the smallest card size.

## First approval pack

Six representative suspects, two items, case-board scene, eliminated/culprit states, and phone/tablet/desktop deduction screens.
