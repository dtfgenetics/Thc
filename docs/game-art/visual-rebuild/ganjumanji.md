# Ganjumanji: The Lost Grower's Temple — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route target: `/games/ganjumanji/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/GANJUMANJI-The-Lost-Grower-s-Temple`
Rebuild class: full art/world-building pass over the existing five-region Phaser campaign.

## Visual goal

An original top-down cannabis-fantasy temple expedition with five visually distinct regions: Root Halls, Sunken Archive, Vault Heart, Glasshouse Ruins and Seed Throne. Relics, pressure surges, guardians, sanctuaries and hazards must read instantly on the tile field.

## Required assets

- logo/key art/world map.
- explorer/player sprite animations.
- five region tile/environment kits.
- 10 relic-seed visuals or coherent tiered relic family.
- guardian, trap, surge and hazard families.
- resin ward, expedition kit and sanctuary/checkpoint assets.
- doors/passages/region transitions.
- objective, health, next-surge, inventory and journal HUD.
- pickup/damage/ward/block/region-unlock/win/loss effects.
- results and campaign-completion scene.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait/landscape must preserve the tile field and touch D-pad/action reachability; journal and deeper records stay behind drawers. Tablet: larger map field + visible compact journal/objectives. Desktop: expanded world viewport with low-chrome HUD and keyboard support.

## First approval pack

One hero sprite, visual sheet for all five regions, representative guardian/relic/sanctuary, HUD and phone/tablet/desktop exploration screens.
