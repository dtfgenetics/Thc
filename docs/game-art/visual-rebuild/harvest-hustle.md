# Harvest Hustle — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/harvest-hustle/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new arcade renderer over useful timer/queue/scoring loop.

## Visual goal

A fast, satisfying harvest-room shift with animated batches moving through four workstations, strong combo feedback and readable mistakes under time pressure.

## Required assets

- logo/key art and harvest-room environment.
- conveyor/batch sprites.
- four workstation art sets with idle/active/error/complete states.
- worker/mascot animation where useful.
- timer, combo, score and queue HUD.
- correct action, mistake, jam, completion and shift-end effects.
- shift intro/results scenes.

## Responsive composition

Phone landscape: workstations become large reachable zones around the center queue; timer/score top edge. Tablet: full conveyor + stations visible. Desktop: wider production room with stations arranged spatially but still one-glance readable.

## First approval pack

Full shift scene, four station designs, batch sprites, feedback effects and phone/tablet/desktop gameplay frames.
