# Terpocalypse: Grow Room From Hell — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/terpocalypse/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Terpocalapse`
Rebuild class: near-total visual/runtime rebuild; salvage useful data/mechanics.

## Visual goal

An original exaggerated retro-FPS grow-room apocalypse. Classic fast-shooter readability is inspiration only; no copied franchise art, HUD, enemies, weapons, textures or levels.

## Required assets

- logo/key art and level-loading treatments.
- first-person Trim Shears, pH Blaster, Neem Cannon and Trichome Burst weapon systems.
- Spider Mite Swarm, Powdery Mildew Ghoul, Nute Burn Imp plus future enemy/miniboss/boss families with idle/move/attack/hit/death states.
- grow-room environment kit: mylar, concrete, trays, reservoirs, lights, ducts, fans, cables, doors, contamination decals, hazards.
- pickups: health, armor, ammo, keys, overdrive and weapons.
- crosshair, health/armor/ammo/key/weapon HUD.
- muzzle flashes, projectiles, impacts, spores, smoke, hit feedback and damage-direction effects.
- extraction/goal marker, pause/settings and death/victory screens.

## Responsive composition

Primary play is landscape. Phone landscape: two-thumb movement/look zones with explicit fire/use buttons; ultra-low HUD density. Tablet landscape: larger virtual-stick spacing and optional inventory strip. Desktop: mouse/pointer-lock or equivalent controls with keyboard; HUD hugs edges and keeps center/lower-middle clear.

## First approval pack

One complete Veg Lab combat scene, one weapon family, three enemy silhouettes, environment material sheet, pickup sheet and phone/tablet/desktop FPS HUD frames.
