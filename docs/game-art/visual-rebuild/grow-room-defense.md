# Grow Room Defense — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/grow-room-defense/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new tactical grow-room renderer over useful round/defense mechanics.

## Visual goal

A readable IPM defense strategy scene where three benches and incoming threats visually communicate plant health, contamination, tool placement and defensive outcomes.

## Required assets

- logo/key art and grow-room environment.
- three detailed bench surfaces.
- healthy/stressed/damaged plant states.
- pest/pathogen threat characters/icons.
- IPM tool cards/icons and placement markers.
- clean/contaminated, deploy, block, damage and recovery animations/VFX.
- round/threat meter, tool tray, bench health/status and victory garden.

## Responsive composition

Phone landscape preferred: three benches stacked or horizontally paged with tool tray on bottom edge; tap bench to focus. Tablet: all benches + full tool tray. Desktop: entire room visible with compact threat/round HUD.

## First approval pack

Grow-room scene, three benches, representative threats/tools, damage/recovery states and responsive gameplay frames.
