# Lost in the Terps — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/lost-in-the-terps/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new mission/puzzle presentation over word-search logic/content.

## Visual goal

A terpene-world expedition where word-search puzzles feel like missions through aromatic botanical environments rather than plain letter grids.

## Required assets

- logo/key art.
- mission-map/environment backgrounds.
- terpene icons and abstract molecule/botanical motifs.
- custom letter-tile styling.
- selection trail, valid word, invalid selection and found-word effects.
- progress relics/mission badges and win portal/exit treatment.
- word-list/journal treatment.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: square grid dominates; word list collapses to compact progress strip/drawer. Tablet: grid plus visible mission word panel. Desktop: puzzle centered inside an illustrated mission scene with side journal.

## First approval pack

One complete mission environment, tile/state system, eight terpene symbols and phone/tablet/desktop puzzle screens.
