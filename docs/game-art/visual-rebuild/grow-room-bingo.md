## Visual goal

A lively event/community bingo card that works equally well on a phone at an event and on a desktop stream/community night.

## Required assets

- logo/mode lockups.
- premium 5×5 board frame and BINGO header.
- 25+ event icons/illustrations.
- free-space treatment.
- dab/stamp/marker states.
- game-mode skins and share-code card.
- winning-line, near-win and celebration effects.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: board fills width with large tap areas and compact controls. Tablet: board + event legend/share area. Desktop: centered board with optional session/event panel to side; never stretch cells excessively.
