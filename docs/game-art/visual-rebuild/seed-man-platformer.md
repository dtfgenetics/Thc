# Seed Man: Grow. Fight. Restore. — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/seed-man-platformer/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: major visual/runtime rebuild; retain campaign/combat/state systems when they remain useful.

## Visual goal

A polished original 2D/2.5D platform adventure with a consistent hero silhouette, expressive enemies, distinct worlds, readable combat and strong phenotype-power transformation feedback.

## Required assets

- canonical Seed Man character turnaround and approved in-game anchor frame.
- base + Plant/Fire/Electric/Ice visual states.
- full animation sets: idle, run, jump, fall, attack, hurt, KO, celebrate, ability-specific actions.
- complete enemy roster and minor-boss/boss animation families.
- five world kits: backgrounds, parallax layers, platforms, hazards, props, collectibles, portals/checkpoints.
- Fire projectile, Electric lightning, Ice freeze and Plant ability VFX.
- boss telegraphs, impacts, damage and defeat effects.
- HUD hearts/health, power timer, collectibles, checkpoint and boss meter.
- world map, level select, pause/settings, title/loading, victory/defeat.
- mobile control graphics.

## Responsive composition

Primary play orientation is landscape. Phone landscape: translucent left movement zone + right action/ability cluster, compact top-edge HUD, no lower-middle obstruction. Tablet landscape: controls move outward with more spacing; HUD can expose extra collectible detail. Desktop: keyboard/gamepad first, touch UI hidden unless pointer type requires it. Portrait entry state may offer rotate/reflow guidance rather than rendering a crushed playfield.

## First approval pack

Hero anchor + four power states, one full world gameplay frame, one enemy sheet, one boss frame, HUD, phone landscape and desktop mockups.
