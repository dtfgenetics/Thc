# High Life: From Bagseed to Legacy — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/high-life/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new illustrated board/journey renderer over reusable turn/event/economy logic.

## Visual goal

A career journey visibly evolving through Underground → Medical → Legal eras. It should feel like traveling through a living illustrated timeline, not a property-board clone.

## Required assets

- High Life logo/key art.
- three panoramic era play surfaces.
- two era-transition scenes.
- player journey markers/pawns.
- eight resource icons: reputation, cash, knowledge, assets, compliance, brand, operations, genetics.
- decision, event, opportunity, warning and milestone card frames.
- preparation gates and era markers.
- location/building illustrations.
- Legacy score crest and final results tableau.

## Responsive composition

Phone: vertical journey path with current decision/card full-width and resources in a compact expandable ribbon. Tablet portrait: vertical board + contextual card pane; landscape: board left, current decision right. Desktop: wide illustrated journey with card/action panel anchored to one edge. Never shrink a wide board into unreadable phone scale.

## First approval pack

Three-era triptych, one representative board segment, event card, eight resource icons, phone/tablet/desktop gameplay frames.
