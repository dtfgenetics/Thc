# Bud or Bluff — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route: `/games/bud-or-bluff/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc`
Rebuild class: new party-game/show presentation over reusable room/round/scoring systems.

## Visual goal

A fast social strain-name bluffing show with tactile cards, confident reveals, strong typography and celebratory reactions. Avoid implying fictional strain art is botanical evidence.

## Required assets

- logo and reveal-stage key art.
- neutral name-card front/back.
- BUD and BLUFF choice systems with icon + shape differences, not color alone.
- reveal stamps, correct/wrong effects, Double Hit treatment.
- player avatar frames, scoreboard, voting tokens, reaction stickers.
- lobby/room code, timer, winner podium/results.
- optional abstract botanical/bud textures not labeled as factual cultivar photos.

## Responsive composition

Phone portrait: name card center, huge BUD/BLUFF controls bottom, opponent/score avatars in compact top strip. Tablet: reveal stage with player rail. Desktop: central card/stage and perimeter player pods; chat/reactions remain secondary.

## First approval pack

Neutral card, both choice/reveal states, six avatar frames, round stage and phone/tablet/desktop gameplay screens.
