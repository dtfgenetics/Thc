# THC RPG — The First Seed — Visual Rebuild Brief

Route target: `/games/thc-rpg/`
Current owner: `dtfgenetics/Thc-rpg`
Rebuild class: full RPG art/UI pass over useful six-chapter cultivation/phenotype/keeper systems.

## Visual goal

A cultivation RPG where the grow room, genetics, phenotype hunt, keeper selection, clone proof and later trials are experienced as a world—not a stack of forms.

## Required assets

- logo/key art and campaign map.
- main player, NPC cast and dialogue portraits.
- multiple grow-room/environment upgrade states.
- Blue Mango, Blue Bubblegum, Mango Bubbles and Zestberry plant/genetics visual families represented as fictionalized game art rather than unsupported botanical certainty.
- equipment, inventory, currency and quest icons.
- plant growth/health/stress/hydration/harvest visual states.
- phenotype trait cards, Keeper badge, cutting/clone propagation visuals and Grow Journal treatment.
- location/travel scenes and chapter transition art.
- environmental-control HUD for temperature, humidity, light, pH and EC.
- XP/level/reward, harvest, keeper, unlock and completion VFX.

## Responsive composition

Phone: grow room/world dominates; environmental controls become contextual bottom sheet; quest and journal collapse. Tablet: world/grow room plus a persistent narrow control/journal rail. Desktop: larger environment with compact side controls; avoid dashboard grids occupying the main scene.

## First approval pack

One grow-room chapter scene, player + NPC portrait pair, plant-state sheet, environmental HUD, Keeper/Grow Journal screen and phone/tablet/desktop compositions.
