# THC Encyclopedia Consensus Source Spot-Check

Generated for the canonical 420 architecture implementation branch.

## Scope

This is a targeted source audit note, not a production claim-evidence approval. The encyclopedia release report still treats claim-level evidence as pending unless it is represented in the controlled evidence tracking data.

## Findings

- Nutrient-disorder and diagnostic entries should prioritize controlled cannabis-specific studies over generic crop symptom tables. Cockson et al. characterized nutrient disorder symptoms in `Cannabis sativa` using silica sand culture, induced nutrient omissions/toxicities, MRML tissue analysis, and staged symptom photographs [1]. Saloner and Bernstein provide controlled nitrogen-response evidence under long photoperiod, including physiological, biomass, photosynthetic, and ionome effects across nitrogen concentrations [2].
- Genetics, genomics, chemotype, and breeding entries should keep marker/gene claims bounded. Grassa et al. support the encyclopedia's caution that cannabinoid synthase loci help explain THC:CBD ratio, while overall cannabinoid content/potency involves additional genomic regions and should not be reduced to one marker [3].
- Disease, viroid, biosecurity, sanitation, and propagation entries should treat HLVd as a testing and systems-control issue, not a visual diagnosis. Punja et al. report RT-PCR/RT-qPCR-based evidence for spread through stock plants, cuttings, recirculated solution, roots, reproductive tissues, pollen, and seeds, with sanitation and meristem culture findings that belong in evidence-bounded management guidance [4].
