# THC Video Academy — Documentary asset capture and acceptance standard

Status: production specification, not evidence that any footage has been obtained or approved.

## House style

Real botanical photography first; scientific illustrations second; decorative AI art never presented as real microscopy or specimen evidence. Vertical master 1080×1920 H.264/AAC, horizontal course edition 1920×1080 as a separate export. Favor deliberate 4–8-second shots, subtle motion, accurate anatomical labels, restrained brand watermark, clear contrast, audio intelligible at mobile volume, and no misleading biology.

**Human narration only.** No synthetic / cloned / AI voiceover, including the old THC drafts. Capture 48 kHz 24-bit WAV from a human speaker. Preserve raw source audio and an edited master; do not overprocess vocal dynamics. Final AAC mux requires an actual human recording.

## Procurement and provenance requirements

For each shot record creator/copyright owner, source URL or original capture identifier, commercial/social redistribution rights, signed release/permission where applicable, date, original filename, SHA-256, specimen provenance, stage, and whether image/video is **camera footage, microscopy, timelapse, or schematic illustration**. An AI-generated illustration must be disclosed as illustration. Do not download watermarked stock previews into the release build or assume YouTube/Google search results grant rights.

## Scene-level QA

1. Does footage actually show the scientific stage described by the script?
2. Is camera motion genuine or transparently editorial? No misleading simulated microscopy.
3. Can on-screen labels be read on a 6-inch phone with subtitles enabled?
4. Does the scene accurately reflect recorded observation instead of guessing environmental measurements?
5. Are audible words human-recorded and consistent with the reviewed script?
6. Is every evidence file nonempty and associated with the correct final asset hash?
7. Does the SRT match words and fit the duration with no overlap?
8. Is the educational context adult-only, non-commercial, and correct for the THC Discord profile CTA?

No publication until independent science review, editorial approval, cleared rights, verified genuine human narration, validated exported file and a real Metricool read-back. Existing 12 old synthetic-narration posts remain drafts.
