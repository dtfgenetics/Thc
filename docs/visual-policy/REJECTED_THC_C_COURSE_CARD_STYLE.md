## Important distinction

Cream, green, black, navy, boxed panels, charts, and academic layouts are **not** rejected by themselves. Those elements are present in the approved THC education visual language. Rejection is based on execution quality and whether the asset matches the approved infographic standard in `APPROVED_THC_EDUCATION_INFOGRAPHIC_STYLE.md`.

## Rejected fingerprint

Reject an education visual when one or more of these dominate the finished asset:
- simplistic, low-detail, clip-art-like or generic flat plant artwork used as the primary teaching visual;
- tiny academic labels or diagrams that become unreadable at normal page/display size;
- sparse course-card composition with large areas that do not carry useful information;
- repeated generic boxes/icons that substitute for subject-specific diagrams, photography, charts, or botanical illustration;
- obvious placeholder or missing-image containers;
- weak visual hierarchy, inconsistent alignment, cramped or overflowing copy, or arbitrary spacing;
- generic AI-looking imagery that does not accurately represent the biological structure/process being taught;
- low-resolution or visibly soft imagery in a final production infographic;
- decorative cannabis imagery that does not teach the topic;
- factual labels, units, diagrams, or relationships that are inaccurate, unsupported, or visually misleading.

## Replacement rule

- Product cards: approved strain/product imagery or a neutral branded placeholder only.
- Education / Atlas: use an explicitly approved THC education infographic, high-quality botanical/scientific visual, chart, diagram, or clean non-image layout.
- Never replace a rejected visual with unrelated stock art merely to fill the slot.
- Do not republish rejected basenames through workflows, import manifests, product cards, seed cards, education pages, WordPress media sync, or format/extension conversions.
- A filename prefix or color palette never constitutes approval. New visuals remain draft until explicitly reviewed/approved.
