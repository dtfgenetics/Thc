## Editorial source standards

- References should support the technical claim instead of decorating the caption. Prefer accessible university extension, peer-reviewed plant-physiology or horticulture publications, or qualified textbooks. Record DOI/URL and date checked in the post manifest.
- Examples: VPD must distinguish air-based VPD from leaf-to-air VPD; DLI = PPFD × hours × 0.0036 (assuming constant PPFD); pH availability depends on medium and chemistry; genotype and phenotype terminology should not imply one-to-one trait expression.
- Avoid unverified charts, unsupported absolute claims, promotional product sales/trades or claims that membership has increased when data is unavailable.
- Content must follow platform-specific age and cannabis-content rules.
