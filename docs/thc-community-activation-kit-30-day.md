# THC community activation kit — 30-day pilot

Status: editorial material prepared; NOT published, scheduled, or sent.

## Weekly cadence
- Sunday: "THC Science Sunday" 30–45-minute moderated discussion (time to be confirmed by moderators).
- Monday: a practical observation prompt connected to THC learning materials.
- Wednesday: evidence-versus-assumption discussion and one quiz.
- Friday: educational resource roundup, member question highlights with permission.

## Welcome post (moderator-ready)
**Welcome to THC — Teaching Healthy Cultivation!**
This is an adults-only space to explore plant biology and evidence-based cultivation education. Choose a learning topic, read the rules, and introduce yourself with one plant-science question you're curious about. You can start with light, water, plant nutrition, roots, genetics, or plant-health observations. Please don't share private personal information. No sales, trades, sourcing, or transaction requests for regulated products.

## First event: THC Science Sunday
**Topic:** Why leaf color alone cannot diagnose a plant problem.
**Format:** 5 minutes introductions; 10 minutes on observations vs hypotheses; 15 minutes comparing environmental and nutritional variables; 10 minutes moderated questions; 5 minutes recap.
**Materials:** illustrative observations, documented conditions, reference links to the Academy/Encyclopedia.
**Moderator preflight:** choose date/time and time zone; confirm moderators; review Discord and local rules; announce after confirmation; no claims of professional diagnosis.

## First educational challenge
**Challenge:** "Observation or inference?"
Share three *non-identifying* observations of a plant image or record, one plausible hypothesis, and the additional measurement that could support or reject it.
**Success metric:** distinct opted-in participants and substantive replies, not raw reactions.
**Evaluation:** helpfulness, evidence and uncertainty; no prizes conditioned on invitations.

## Seven short-form video scripts — draft only
1. **Yellow leaves aren't a diagnosis.** Hook: "One symptom can have several causes." Explain why leaf age and location, irrigation, pH and root-zone conditions matter. CTA: "Continue the evidence discussion in THC."
2. **What PPFD measures.** Hook: "A bright-looking lamp isn't a measurement." Explain incident photosynthetic photon flux density, measurement location and units. CTA: "Explore the learning tools, then join the conversation."
3. **pH vs nutrient availability.** Hook: "Adding more nutrients isn't always the answer." Define pH and explain that availability depends on media and conditions. CTA: "Bring your questions to THC."
4. **VPD without the guesswork.** Hook: "Humidity alone doesn't describe the plant's drying environment." Explain the role of leaf temperature and vapor pressure difference without prescribing a universal setpoint. CTA: "Review the VPD education resources."
5. **Genotype and phenotype.** Hook: "Related plants don't always look identical." Define genotype, phenotype, environment and variation. CTA: "Discuss plant genetics in THC."
6. **Why observations need context.** Hook: "One photograph can't tell you everything." Demonstrate logging stage, lighting, recent changes and measurements. CTA: "Learn with the THC community."
7. **What an evidence-based answer sounds like.** Hook: "A good answer includes uncertainty." Compare an unsupported claim with a testable explanation, and link to original references. CTA: "Join THC's educational discussions."
