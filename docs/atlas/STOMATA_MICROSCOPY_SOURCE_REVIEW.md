# Plant Atlas Stomata Microscopy Source Review

Updated: 2026-09-23

## Goal

Fill `PA-STOMA-001` with a defensible Cannabis-specific microscopy reference. This slot must remain empty until the source is suitable for public redistribution, scientifically identified, and available at useful resolution. Do not substitute generic dicot stomata and do not use AI-generated imagery as measured microscopy.

## Candidate A — Parsons et al. 2019, Frontiers in Plant Science

Article: *Polyploidization for the Genetic Improvement of Cannabis sativa*  
DOI: 10.3389/fpls.2019.00476  
License: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)

Figure 3 includes nail-polish impressions of stomata on the abaxial surface of mature Cannabis sativa fan leaves. Panels C and D show diploid and tetraploid material with 12 µm scale bars.

Status: **scientifically appropriate / source-resolution review still needed**

Why it is promising:
- Cannabis-specific.
- Abaxial mature fan-leaf tissue is identified.
- Scale bar is present.
- Open CC BY reuse terms are compatible with adaptation when attribution is preserved.

Current limitation:
- The directly accessible Frontiers web composite inspected during this review is 800×750 px for the entire A–D figure. That does not meet the Atlas production standard for a standalone microscopy master.
- Do not upscale the web composite and call it high-resolution measured evidence.
- Locate the publisher/archival high-resolution figure file before registering PA-STOMA-001.

## Candidate B — Lavie et al. 2024, Plant Methods

Article: *Optimizing cannabis cultivation: an efficient in vitro system for flowering induction*  
DOI: 10.1186/s13007-024-01265-5

Figure 3 contains Cannabis-specific stomatal SEM/light microscopy with scale bars.

Status: **not approved for Atlas reuse**

Reason:
- The version-of-record rights statement is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.
- The Atlas should not depend on non-commercial/no-derivatives material for a public-facing production asset that may need cropping, annotation, or integration into a commercial site.
- Keep as a scientific reference, not a production media source.
