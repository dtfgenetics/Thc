# Strain Showdown

**Strain Showdown** is an original cannabis-themed trading-card battle system organized around eight strain families. This directory owns the machine-readable roster plan, family identities, rules data, browser-prototype code, and validation.

## Locked families

Kush, Haze, Skunk, Gas, Cookies, Fruit, Purple, Frost.

## Locked card progression

Each family targets 12 strain cards: 6 Tier 1 Base, 4 Tier 2 Select, and 2 Tier 3 Elite, for a 96-strain core roster.

## Battle model

- **Vigor** is the primary defensive stat.
- **Power** is the primary attacking stat.
- Strains progress through three stages/tiers.
- Higher-stage summons require sacrifices or equivalent advancement cost.
- Family identities should create distinct strategic play without making one family strictly superior.

## Current status

`canonical-preproduction` — the roster architecture and family identities are locked, while individual card names, exact values, effects, art, and tournament rules still require balancing and review.
