# Grow Room Defense

Production target: **dtfseeds.com**

Grow Room Defense is a turn-based IPM strategy game. Three plant benches face deterministic pest and disease pressure over 12 rounds. Players choose a defense tool and a bench, then learn whether the counter was strong, supportive, or mismatched.

## First playable vertical slice

- three plant lanes with independent health;
- deterministic six-character defense codes;
- 12 reproducible threat rounds;
- eight pest/disease/diagnosis threat profiles;
- seven broad IPM tools;
- strong, supportive, mismatch, and empty-lane outcomes;
- persistent unresolved pressure and scaled plant damage;
- score, resolved-threat count, total damage, and action history;
- a perfect-play path where strong counterplay can prevent damage;
- self-hosted responsive browser route with original CSS game-board art.

## Education boundary

The game teaches broad IPM concepts such as scouting, sanitation, monitoring, biological control, moisture management, airflow, and environmental review. It intentionally does **not** provide pesticide recipes, mixing rates, application rates, or treatment instructions.
