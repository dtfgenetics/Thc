Reference bands

Approx. VPDGeneral interpretation
< 0.8 kPaLow evaporative demand
0.8–1.2 kPaModerate
1.2–1.6 kPaHigher demand
> 1.6 kPaVery high demand

These are broad environmental bands, not stage-specific prescriptions. Cultivar, root-zone status, light, airflow, CO₂, and acclimation all affect plant response.
