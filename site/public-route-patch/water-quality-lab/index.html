Water report

Interpretation workflow

1. Preserve the reportKeep lab, date, source, units and sampling point.
2. Separate pH from alkalinitypH describes the current acid/base condition; alkalinity describes acid-neutralizing capacity and buffering load.
3. Read ions with ECEC reflects total ionic conductivity, not which ions are present.
4. Re-test trendsDo not make a correction from one isolated number without confirming the source and method.
