Root-zone check

Why record it

Root-zone temperature can diverge from room temperature because of irrigation temperature, container mass, floor contact, radiation, airflow and evaporative cooling. Trend the root-zone value directly.

Temperature and EC are context, not oxygen measurements. Root-zone temperature can influence gas solubility and root metabolism, but this tool does not infer dissolved oxygen from temperature or EC. Use an appropriate oxygen measurement method when oxygen itself is the question.
