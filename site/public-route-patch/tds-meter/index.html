Two meters can display different ppm values for the same conductivity if they use different conversion factors. Preserve EC and units whenever possible.

What EC tells you

Electrical conductivity reflects how readily the solution conducts current. It is influenced by dissolved ions but does not identify which ions are present or whether the nutrient balance is appropriate.

Method and temperature matter

  • Keep the meter clean and calibrated as specified.
  • Record sample temperature or compensation method when important.
  • Use the same sample method when comparing trends.

Keep the context

Save water source, nutrient recipe, pH, irrigation event, sample type, plant stage, and meter scale beside the reading. A number without method and context is difficult to compare later.

Educational conversion and interpretation tool. Use calibrated instruments and preserve the original EC, units, sample method, and meter convention in cultivation records.
