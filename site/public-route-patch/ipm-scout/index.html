Scouting principles

Repeat the same routeTrap/zone IDs make counts comparable across dates.
Observation ≠ diagnosisRecord what is visible before assigning a pest, disease or deficiency name.
Thresholds belong to the user/programThe tool can flag an entered threshold without pretending one universal pest threshold applies everywhere.
Trend the countStable, rising and falling counts are more useful than isolated numbers.
