Diagnosing Deficiency vs Toxicity · Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Core rule

Describe the pattern before naming the cause.

Uniform yellowing, interveinal yellowing on old leaves, and pale new growth with green veins are three completely different situations. The pattern tells you far more than the colour.

Mobile vs immobile

Old tissue or new tissue narrows the list by half.

Mobile nutrients (N, Mg, K, P, S) deficiency appears on old leaves first. Immobile (Ca, Fe, Mn, B, Zn) always show on new growth first.
