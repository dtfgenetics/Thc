Chlorosis Pattern Checklist

Identify which chlorosis pattern is present and what it points to. Yellowing pattern and location determine the differential — not the colour alone.

All Field Cards Diagnostics Protocol
Core rule

The chlorosis pattern is the diagnosis — not the colour.

Uniform yellowing, interveinal yellowing on old leaves, and pale new growth with green veins are three completely different situations with different causes and different correct responses.

Always check first

pH before any nutrient addition.

Most chlorosis symptoms are pH lockout — the nutrient is present but unavailable. Adding it at the wrong pH makes no difference and may create antagonism.
