Deficiency vs Lockout Comparison Card

Use this card when symptoms look like missing nutrients but uptake may be limited by pH, EC buildup, water stress, low oxygen, damaged roots, or environment.

Print / Save PDFOpen Nutrient Uptake Lesson

True deficiency clues

  • Feed history may be too light for stage and plant demand.
  • Symptoms match mobile or immobile nutrient behavior.
  • pH, EC, watering, and roots do not show major uptake blockers.
  • Progression is consistent with the nutrient pattern and plant stage.

Lockout / uptake-limit clues

  • Feed is present but pH or EC is outside useful range.
  • Root zone is saturated, compacted, salty, cold, damaged, or low oxygen.
  • Multiple nutrient-looking symptoms appear at once.
  • Symptoms worsen after more feeding instead of improving.
