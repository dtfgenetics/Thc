Core rule

The edge of the necrosis tells you more than the dead tissue.

Sharp yellow halo → likely pathogen. Gradual fade → nutritional or environmental. Abrupt edge with no halo → contact damage or heat.

Spreading vs stable

Active spread means an ongoing cause — stable means historical.

Spreading necrosis with a living yellow border requires faster response. Stable necrosis that is not progressing is residual from a past event.

Assessment checklist

✓Check
☐Is necrosis spreading actively or stable?
☐Does it have a yellow/green border around the dead tissue?
☐Are other plants affected in the same room?
☐Is it on old growth, new growth, or both?
☐Checked leaf underside with 60× loupe for pest evidence?
☐Root condition checked?
☐Photographed before removing affected leaves?
