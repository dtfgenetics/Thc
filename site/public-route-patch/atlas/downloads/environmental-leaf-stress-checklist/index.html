Environmental Leaf Stress Checklist

Identify environmental stress on leaves by canopy position and time-of-day correlation. Environmental symptoms are distributed by proximity to the stressor — not by leaf age.

All Field Cards Diagnostics Protocol
Core rule

Environmental symptoms are distributed by position — not leaf age.

Heat and light stress appear on the uppermost leaves closest to the source. Cold stress appears on the lowest leaves and root zone. Wind damage is directional. This distinguishes environmental from nutritional causes, which follow old-vs-new tissue mobility rules regardless of canopy position.

Measure at the symptom site

Canopy temperature and PPFD differ from room sensor readings.

The environment the plant experiences at the canopy top is different from the room average. Measure temperature, humidity, and PPFD at the actual location of the symptoms before concluding a cause.
