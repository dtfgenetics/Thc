Cannabis Plant Anatomy · Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Core rule

Environmental symptoms are distributed by position — not leaf age.

Heat and light stress appear on uppermost leaves. Cold stress appears on the lowest leaves. Wind damage is directional. Nutritional stress follows old-vs-new tissue rules regardless of canopy position — this is the key distinction.

Measure at the symptom site

Canopy temp and PPFD differ from room sensors.

The environment the plant experiences at the top of the canopy is different from the room average. Measure at the actual symptom location before concluding a cause.
