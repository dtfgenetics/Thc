Nutrient Uptake & Root Zone Chemistry · Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Core rule

Check pH before adding any nutrient.

Most apparent deficiencies are pH lockout — the nutrient is present but unavailable. Adding it at the wrong pH changes nothing and may create antagonism with other elements.

EC interpretation

Runoff EC reveals what is accumulating in the substrate.

Runoff EC significantly higher than input indicates salt buildup. Flush with plain water when runoff EC consistently exceeds input by more than 0.5–1.0 mS/cm.

EC problem response

High runoff EC

Salt accumulation — flush protocol.

  1. Irrigate with 2–3× container volume at correct pH
  2. Collect and measure runoff EC at the end
  3. Target: runoff EC within 0.5 mS/cm of input water
  4. Allow 20–30% dryback before resuming nutrient feed
  5. Resume at 50–75% of previous EC, build back over 2–3 feeds
pH correction

After correcting pH — wait before adding nutrients.

  1. Correct pH to the middle of the target range
  2. Wait 3–5 days before adding any element
  3. Observe whether symptoms are stabilising
  4. Recovery from lockout begins within 5–7 days at correct pH
  5. Only supplement the specific element if symptoms continue after correct pH for 1 week
