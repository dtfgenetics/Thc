pH & EC Troubleshooting Card

Diagnose root zone chemistry problems from pH and EC readings. Out-of-range pH is the most common cause of nutrient deficiency symptoms regardless of substrate nutrient content.

All Field Cards Diagnostics Protocol
Core rule

Check pH before adding any nutrient.

The correct nutrient at the wrong pH is unavailable. Adding it at a locked-out pH does nothing to the deficiency symptoms and may create antagonism with other elements. Every visual nutrient deficiency diagnosis must start with a pH test.

EC interpretation

Runoff EC tells you what is accumulating in the substrate.

Runoff EC significantly higher than input EC indicates salt buildup — nutrients and minerals accumulating in the root zone rather than being absorbed. Flush with plain water when runoff EC exceeds input by more than 0.5–1.0 mS/cm consistently.

EC problem indicators

What runoff EC means.

  • Runoff EC much higher than input: salt buildup — flush with 2× container volume plain water at correct pH
  • Runoff EC lower than input: substrate may be buffering (coco initial use) or plant under-feeding — check EC of both and review stage targets
  • Rising runoff EC over multiple feeds: accumulation trend — reduce feed frequency or EC until runoff stabilises
  • pH swinging with EC: large pH swings often accompany EC swings — flush and restart at lower EC
