Leaf Curl Pattern Guide

← All Field Cards
Core rule

Curl direction is the first observation — before texture or colour.

Upward curl and downward curl have different causes. Assess direction plus which tissue is affected before measuring anything.

Time check

Does it appear during lights-on and recover at lights-off?

Transient curl that appears at peak VPD and recovers toward lights-off is stomatal. Curl persisting through the dark period is structural or nutritional.
