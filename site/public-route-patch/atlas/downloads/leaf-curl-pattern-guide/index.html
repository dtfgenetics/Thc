Leaf Curl Pattern Guide

Identify curl direction, tissue age, and time-of-day correlation to determine whether the cause is environmental, nutritional, or pest-related.

All Field Cards Diagnostics Protocol
Core rule

Curl direction is the first observation — not the texture or colour.

Upward curl and downward curl have completely different causes. Assessing direction, then which tissue is affected, narrows the differential list before any measurement is needed.

Time check

Does it appear during lights-on and recover at lights-off?

Curl that appears during the high-VPD midday window and recovers toward lights-off is stomatal closure — not a structural or nutritional problem. Curl that persists into the dark period indicates a different, non-transient cause.

Time-of-day assessment

Observation windows

When to check.

  • □ At lights-on: note initial leaf position
  • □ At peak VPD (1–3h after lights-on): note if curl appears or worsens
  • □ At lights-off: note if curl recovers
  • □ 1h into dark period: if still curled, non-transient cause

Transient curl (appears, then recovers) = environmental/stomatal. Persistent curl = structural or nutritional.

New growth distortion checklist

Check with 60× loupe before assuming deficiency.

  • □ Inspect growing tip with 60× minimum
  • □ Check for broad mites: glossy surface, thick texture, distorted leaf shape
  • □ Check for russet mites: silvery base of stems, moving up the plant
  • □ Distinguish from Ca/B deficiency: pest treatment vs root zone adjustment
