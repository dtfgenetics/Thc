Pest Damage on Leaves

Pest feeding produces distinctive patterns that differ from nutrient, environmental, and pathogen damage. Accurate identification requires a hand lens, knowledge of feeding mechanics, and inspection of the correct surfaces.

← Nutrient SymptomsModule IndexModule IndexEnvironmental Stress →
Core rule

Always inspect the leaf underside with a hand lens first.

Spider mites, russet mites, thrips, and aphids concentrate on the lower surface. A symptom that looks nutritional from above may have obvious pest evidence visible in 30 seconds with a 60× loupe on the underside.

Scout early

Detection before visible damage requires looking before symptoms appear.

Russet and broad mites cause symptoms weeks after establishment. Scout weekly — inspect new growth, mid-canopy, and leaf undersides, not just fan leaves.
