Leaf Curl

Leaf curl is the leaf's physical response to stress — but direction, affected tissue age, and time-of-day correlation all point to different causes. Curl direction is the first observation to make.

← NecrosisModule IndexModule IndexNutrient Symptoms →
Core rule

Curl direction is the first diagnostic signal.

Upward curl and downward curl have completely different causes. Direction plus tissue age narrows the cause substantially before any measurement.

Time check

Does it appear during lights-on and recover at lights-off?

Transient curl that appears at peak VPD and recovers toward lights-off is stomatal — not a structural problem. Curl that persists into the dark period indicates a non-transient cause.
