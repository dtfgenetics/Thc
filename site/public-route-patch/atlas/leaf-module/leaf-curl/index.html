Leaf Curl

Leaf curl describes posture and shape changes such as tacoing, clawing, drooping, twisting, cupping, and canoeing. Posture changes often point to environment or root-zone stress.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext: Nutrient Symptoms

Pattern language

  • Tacoing/canoeing: edges curl upward.
  • Clawing: tips curl downward.
  • Drooping: leaf loses turgor and hangs.
  • Twisting: uneven growth or localized stress.

What to check

Check VPD, leaf temperature, light intensity, fan blast, substrate moisture, root oxygen, EC strength, recent irrigation, and whether curl changes between lights-on and lights-off.

Common look-alikes

Heat/light stress, low humidity/high VPD, overwatering, underwatering, root-zone oxygen issues, nutrient strength, wind stress, pest damage, and genetic leaf shape can look similar.
