Field checklist

  • Separate old-growth symptoms from new-growth symptoms.
  • Record mobile-vs-immobile style pattern without forcing a diagnosis.
  • Check whether pH/EC or watering explains availability.
  • Compare feed history against plant stage and demand.

Measurements to pair

  • pH, EC/PPM, feed schedule, source water.
  • Media type, irrigation volume, runoff/dryback.
  • Temperature, RH, VPD, and root health.

Common mistakes

  • Adding supplements before measuring basics.
  • Ignoring root stress or lockout-like conditions.
  • Confusing pest damage and environmental stress for nutrients.

Grow Doc prompt

Evaluate nutrient-like leaf symptoms with old/new growth, symptom pattern, pH, EC, feed history, irrigation, media, roots, environment, pests, and recent changes. Separate true deficiency from lockout or uptake limits.
