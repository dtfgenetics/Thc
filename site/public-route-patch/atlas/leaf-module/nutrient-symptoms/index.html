Nutrient Symptoms

Nutrient deficiency and toxicity symptoms follow predictable patterns based on mobility, pH availability, and antagonism. Check pH before adding any element — most apparent deficiencies are lockout, not absence.

← Leaf CurlModule IndexModule IndexPest Damage →
Core rule

Check pH before adding any nutrient.

Most nutrient deficiency symptoms are pH-driven unavailability — not actual absence. Adding the element at the wrong pH does nothing and may create antagonism with others.

Mobility first

Old or new tissue determines the element category.

This single observation cuts the differential list in half. Mobile nutrients show deficiency on old tissue. Immobile nutrients always show on new growth first.
