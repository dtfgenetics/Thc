Necrosis

Necrosis is dead plant tissue. Brown tips, burnt margins, spots, and lesions are evidence of damage, but the cause must be separated from the appearance.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext: Leaf Curl

Pattern language

  • Tip burn: damage starting at the leaflet tips.
  • Marginal necrosis: brown edges or rim-like damage.
  • Spot necrosis: isolated brown or tan spots.
  • Lesions: irregular dead patches, sometimes with halos.

What to check

Record pH, EC, runoff or root-zone strength when available, irrigation timing, media moisture, VPD, light intensity, airflow, temperature swings, pest presence, and whether damage is spreading.

Common look-alikes

Nutrient excess, potassium limitation, calcium-related transport stress, dryback problems, root injury, light/heat stress, pest feeding, foliar spray burn, and leaf disease can all create dead tissue.

Image asset needed

Necrosis Evaluation Card: tip burn, margin burn, spots, lesions, halos, and spreading vs stopped damage.
