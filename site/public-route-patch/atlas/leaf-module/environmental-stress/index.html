Environmental Stress on Leaves

Leaves respond quickly to light, heat, humidity, VPD, airflow, water availability, and root oxygen. Environment can mimic nutrition and pests.

PreviousLeaf ModuleBack to Atlas

Visual reference slot

Needed asset: Environmental Leaf Stress Checklist mapping canopy position, light zone, fan path, VPD status, moisture status, and symptom timing.

Field checklist

  • Map symptoms to canopy location and equipment position.
  • Record whether stress worsens during lights-on peak.
  • Compare leaves near fans, lights, walls, humidifiers, and vents.

Measurements to pair

  • Air temperature, RH, VPD, leaf temperature.
  • PPFD/DLI, photoperiod, airflow, fan direction.
  • Water status, dryback, root-zone oxygen.

Common mistakes

  • Diagnosing nutrient issues before checking environment.
  • Using room averages and ignoring microclimates.
  • Changing too many variables at once.

Grow Doc prompt

Evaluate environmental stress by mapping symptom location to light, heat, RH, VPD, fan path, water status, root oxygen, equipment position, and timing across the day.
