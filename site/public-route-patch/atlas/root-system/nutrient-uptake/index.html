Nutrient Uptake

Nutrient uptake depends on root health, available ions, pH, EC, water movement, oxygen, temperature, and media behavior. A plant can show nutrient-like symptoms even when nutrients are present.

Leaf Nutrient SymptomspH & EC Card

What to learn

  • Roots absorb nutrients from the solution around them.
  • pH influences which nutrients remain available.
  • High EC can increase osmotic stress and reduce water uptake.
  • Low oxygen, saturation, damage, or cold roots can reduce uptake.
  • Deficiency, lockout, and root stress can look similar above ground.
Rule: Do not correct nutrient symptoms without checking root-zone context.

Measurements to pair

Feed pH, runoff/slurry pH, feed EC, runoff EC, substrate moisture, irrigation timing, media type, root-zone temperature, and recent feed changes.
