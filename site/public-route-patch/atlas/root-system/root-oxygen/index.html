Root Oxygen

Roots need oxygen for respiration and active uptake. Saturated, compacted, cold, or poorly aerated media can create deficiency-like leaf symptoms even when water and nutrients are present.

Water UptakeRoot-Zone Diagnostics

What to learn

  • Roots require oxygen to support respiration and uptake.
  • Overwatering often means oxygen imbalance, not just too much water.
  • Compaction reduces air-filled pore space.
  • Low oxygen can slow growth, reduce uptake, and invite root stress.
  • Media structure and irrigation timing control oxygen availability.
