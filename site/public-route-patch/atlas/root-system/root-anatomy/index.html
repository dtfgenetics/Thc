Root System Module

Root Anatomy

Root anatomy explains how the hidden half of the plant supports water uptake, nutrient access, oxygen demand, anchoring, and stress recovery. Healthy roots are usually bright, actively branching, and supported by an oxygenated root zone.

Core anatomy

What to learn

  • Primary roots anchor early growth and establish direction.
  • Lateral roots expand the absorption network.
  • Root hairs increase surface area and are easy to damage.
  • Root tips are active growth zones and indicators of root vigor.
  • Color, smell, branching, and texture help separate healthy roots from stressed roots.
