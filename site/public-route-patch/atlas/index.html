Developmental context

The same tissue means different things at different stages

Development changes source-sink relationships, architecture, reproductive priorities, tissue age, water demand, and the meaning of visible symptoms.

1SeedEmbryo protected inside the seed coat.
2GerminationImbibition, radicle emergence, shoot establishment.
3SeedlingCotyledons transition toward true-leaf photosynthesis.
4Vegetative growthRapid leaf, root, stem, node, and branch expansion.
5Flower transitionMeristem identity and source-sink priorities change.
6Reproductive developmentFlowers, pollen, fertilization, seed, and maturation pathways.
⌕

Observation rule: a visible pattern is evidence, not proof of a single cause. Confirm location, progression, developmental stage, environment, root-zone conditions, pest/pathogen evidence, and recent changes before acting.
