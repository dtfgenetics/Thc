Shoot apical meristem

The shoot apex continuously generates new shoot tissues. Removing or damaging it can change dominance relationships and redistribute growth toward lateral buds.

Observation

What to record

  • Node number and internode length at repeatable positions.
  • Branch symmetry, vigor, angle, and axillary bud activity.
  • Canopy PPFD distribution and recent pruning or training.
  • Whether architecture is consistent across comparable plants.
Interpretation

Avoid single-cause conclusions

Internode length and branch vigor are context-dependent. Genetics, light distribution, developmental stage, and recent manipulation can all produce similar architecture.

Evidence boundary: one unusual node does not establish a diagnosis.
