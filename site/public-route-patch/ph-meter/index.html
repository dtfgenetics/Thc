pH Meter

Interpret a pH reading

A pH value is logarithmic. A one-unit change represents a tenfold change in hydrogen-ion activity.

Common reference windows

SystemReference window
Soilless / hydroponic nutrient solutionabout 5.5–6.5
Container soil / peat-based mixesabout 6.0–7.0

Actual targets depend on substrate, fertilizer chemistry, irrigation water alkalinity, crop stage, and the nutrient program. Follow product and substrate guidance when it is more specific.

Calibration check

  • Use fresh, uncontaminated buffer solutions.
  • Calibrate at the frequency required by the meter manufacturer.
  • Rinse the probe between standards and samples.
  • Store the electrode in the manufacturer-recommended storage solution, not distilled water.

How to use the reading

Record the reading with date, water source, nutrient mix, EC/TDS, temperature, and whether the sample is input solution or root-zone runoff. A single pH number is most useful when tied to trend and context.

Educational reference tool. Use calibrated instruments and manufacturer instructions for decisions involving water chemistry, nutrients, or equipment.
