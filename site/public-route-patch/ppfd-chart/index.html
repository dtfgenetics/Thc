Interpretation

Measure the light distribution, not just the center number.

PPFD is most useful when the measurement method is repeatable and the numbers are interpreted alongside DLI, canopy geometry, leaf temperature, VPD, irrigation capacity and plant response.

Use a quantum sensor when accuracy matters

PPFD is a photon-based measurement over the photosynthetically active waveband. A purpose-built quantum sensor is the appropriate reference instrument. Lux-based conversions are spectrum-dependent estimates and should be labeled as estimates.

Hold geometry constant

Record fixture height, dimming state, sensor orientation, crop-plane height, grid spacing and reflective boundaries. A map cannot be meaningfully compared later if the measurement geometry changes without being documented.

DLI is dose, not a complete growth recipe

The same DLI can result from different PPFD × photoperiod combinations. Those combinations are not biologically identical because photon rate, dark duration and interacting environmental conditions differ.

Do not chase one universal cannabis target

Published cannabis studies show meaningful responses across broad light ranges, and responses differ with cultivar, environment, acclimation and production objective. Treat research ranges as context rather than as a guaranteed optimum for every grow.
