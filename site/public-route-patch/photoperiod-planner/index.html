Lighting schedule

Ramp assumption: photon flux changes linearly from 0 → entered PPFD at dawn and entered PPFD → 0 at dusk. Each ramp therefore contributes half of its clock duration to full-output-equivalent light hours. Use Light Lab variable blocks when you have a measured non-linear ramp.

Interpretation

Photoperiod controls timing. PPFD describes instantaneous photon flux. DLI describes integrated daily exposure. Changing one does not automatically preserve the others.
