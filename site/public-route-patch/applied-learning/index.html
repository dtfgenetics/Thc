    Measurement School

    Representative canopy sensor placement

    Compare multiple crop-zone measurements before treating one convenient sensor as a room-wide answer.

    1. Choose at least three crop-zone measurement positions.
    2. Record temperature and relative humidity with the same units and time window.
    3. Record sensor ID/location, nearby equipment influence, and whether leaf temperature was measured.
    4. Compare spatial variation before making a room-wide interpretation.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Crop Math
    
    
    DLI from PPFD and photoperiod
    
    
    Daily light integral is photon dose over time. This calculator uses the canonical relation PPFD × hours × 3600 ÷ 1,000,000.
    
    
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
    
      
      
    • The result is a photon-dose calculation, not a universal target.
      • 
      
    • Equal DLI can come from different intensity/time combinations and does not prove equal plant response.
      • 
      
    • Use representative canopy PPFD, not an isolated peak reading.
      • 
      
    • Interpret with spectrum, stage, temperature, water status, CO₂ context, and crop response.

    Same Symptom, Different Cause
    
    
    Yellowing leaves: build a differential first
    
    
    Observed pattern: Older lower leaves are yellowing while newer growth remains comparatively greener.
    
    
    
    
    Evidence that separates the possibilities
    
    
      
      
    • Record tissue age, position, distribution, and progression before naming a cause.
      • 
      
    • Compare feed and root-zone pH/EC using consistent methods.
      • 
      
    • Review irrigation/moisture history and root condition.
      • 
      
    • Compare affected and unaffected zones for temperature, humidity, airflow, and light context.
      • 
      
    • Use laboratory or tissue analysis where appropriate and authorized when stronger evidence is needed.
      • 
    
    
    
    This activity teaches differential reasoning. It does not diagnose a crop or prescribe a nutrient, pesticide, HVAC, electrical, irrigation, or other controlled operational change.
