Measurement method

Use the same reference point, plant posture and time relative to irrigation/lights when possible. Training can change plant height without representing slower biomass accumulation.

Calibrated photo measurement

Load a local photo, calibrate scale by clicking two points across a known reference, then click two points across the plant dimension you want to measure. The image stays in browser memory only and is never written to localStorage; saved records contain only the filename/reference, normalized points, calibration metadata and measurement result.

Calibration is valid only for the same image plane and geometry. Perspective distortion can make measurements inaccurate when the reference and plant dimension are at different depths or angles.
