Measurement boundaries

Weight change, room RH and subjective stem feel are not substitutes for a validated moisture or water-activity measurement when product safety or storage stability must be established.

Record the curve. A sequence of measurements is more useful than one final number.

Vapor-pressure & water-activity context

Room vapor pressure is calculated from measured room temperature and RH. Vapor-pressure deficit here means the air saturation deficit at room temperature; it is not leaf VPD. Measured water activity is reported separately because product aw cannot be inferred from room RH or weight loss alone.
