Transparent recipe workflow

Use the target-versus-achieved worksheet to document the products and masses you actually mixed. Preserve source water, final EC, final pH, volume and notes with every saved mix, then compare repeated runs before changing the recipe. The worksheet remains a transparent mass-balance calculator rather than a black-box feed recommendation.
