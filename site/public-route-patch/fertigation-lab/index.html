Current scope and calculation boundaries

The recipe worksheet currently compares entered targets against the N, P, K, Ca and Mg contributed by up to three products, with P₂O₅ and K₂O converted to elemental values. Sulfur, micronutrients, product solubility limits and concentrate-compatibility constraints are not automatically solved yet, so those inputs must still be reviewed separately. The calculator remains a transparent mass-balance worksheet rather than a black-box feed recommendation.
