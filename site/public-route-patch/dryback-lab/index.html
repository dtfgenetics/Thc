Dryback measurement

What the result means

Weight-based dryback is only comparable when container, substrate, plant and scale method are consistent. The “dry/reference” value must be a defined repeatable reference, not an assumed absolute zero-water state.

Dryback % = water lost since saturation ÷ measured water-holding span.
