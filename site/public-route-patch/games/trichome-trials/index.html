How to judge

Consistency beats guessing.

Read first. Each evidence line maps in order to Structure, Resin, Terps, Yield, Color, Documentation, and Problem Solving.

Score the packet. Review all seven categories. Use the slider or +/- buttons. Mark up to two confidence calls only where the evidence feels strongest.

Review the reveal. Compare your score with the hidden benchmark explanation and see whether your confident calls were exact, near, or off before advancing.

Game boundary: all entries, visuals, observations, and benchmark scores are fictional. Trichome Trials does not provide potency claims, consumption guidance, chemical instructions, or step-by-step cultivation/processing procedures.
