    How Strain Showdown Works

    1

    Play a Base

    Stage 1 Base cards enter an empty lane. They cost 1 Focus.

    2

    Make a Selection

    Replace a same-family Stage 1 card with a Stage 2 Select card. It costs 2 Focus.

    3

    Raise an Elite

    Replace a Stage 2 Select with a Stage 3 Elite. It costs 3 Focus.

    4

    Attack the Lane

    Attacks normally cost 1 Focus. Damage hits the opposing card in that lane; an open lane damages the rival Garden.

    5

    Use Family Identity

    Each family has one prototype passive based on its locked design identity.

    6

    Break the Garden

    Reduce the rival Garden from 20 to 0. If the battle reaches 18 rounds, board strength breaks the tie.

    This browser build is a gameplay prototype. The canonical 96-card roster is locked, while final per-card effect text, balance, art, and print rules remain separate release gates.
