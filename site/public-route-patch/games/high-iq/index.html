    What High IQ actually tests

    Quality model

    Questions that teach after the guess

    01

    Approved question bank

    The current v2.2 bank is machine-validated for IDs, answer mappings, status, audit state, source references, categories and difficulty distribution.

    02

    Difficulty-weighted score

    Easy questions are worth 1 point, Medium 2, Hard 3, and Expert 4, so technical depth matters in the final score.

    03

    Explanation-first feedback

    Every production question includes an explanation and context note so a miss becomes a short lesson instead of a dead end.

    04

    Visible verification

    Source records are attached to questions and shown after an answer is locked. The game makes its evidence trail inspectable.

    Educational use

    High IQ is an adult educational game. Cultivation and plant-health questions teach general principles and diagnostic reasoning; they do not replace legal, medical, electrical, pesticide-label, laboratory, or workplace-safety guidance.
