    Approved question bank

    The v2.2 bank is machine-validated for IDs, answer mappings, status, audit state, source references, category counts, and difficulty distribution.

    Balanced or random runs

    Balanced Mix spreads questions across matching topic and difficulty buckets. Random Mix keeps the classic fully shuffled experience.

    Explanation-first feedback

    Every production question includes an explanation and context note, so a miss becomes a short lesson instead of a dead end.

    04

    Visible verification

    Source records are attached to questions and shown after an answer is locked. High IQ makes its evidence trail inspectable.

    Educational use

    High IQ is an adult educational game. Plant-health and cultivation questions teach general principles and diagnostic reasoning; they do not replace legal, medical, electrical, pesticide-label, laboratory, or workplace-safety guidance.
