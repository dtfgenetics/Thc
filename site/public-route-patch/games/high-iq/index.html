    Quality model

    Questions that teach after the guess

    80 validated questions

    The v2.2 bank spans plant biology, physiology, photobiology, environment, nutrition, irrigation, diagnostics, IPM, genetics, breeding, harvest, and post-harvest topics.

    Difficulty-weighted score

    Easy questions are worth 1 point, Medium 2, Hard 3, and Expert 4. A session score therefore reflects both accuracy and technical difficulty.

    Explanation-first feedback

    Every production question includes an explanation and context note so a wrong answer becomes a short learning step instead of a dead end.

    Visible verification

    Source records are attached to each question and shown after the answer is locked. Sources support the factual claim; they do not replace crop-specific professional judgment.

    Educational use

    High IQ is an adult educational game. Cultivation and plant-health questions teach general principles and diagnostic reasoning; they do not replace legal, medical, electrical, pesticide-label, laboratory, or workplace-safety guidance.
