    Diagnostic rule

    Verify conditions before correcting symptoms.

    Observe: location, timing, posture, color, and which leaves are affected can matter as much as the symptom itself.

    Inspect: use measurements and comparisons to separate look-alike causes.

    Diagnose: choose the explanation that fits the whole pattern, not just one clue.

    Education boundary: plant symptoms can overlap. This game teaches broad diagnostic reasoning and does not replace direct measurement, lab testing, or product-label directions.
