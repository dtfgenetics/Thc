How to Play

Set up players

Choose 2–10 players, enter names, pick Local or Online Room mode.

Roll the dice

The active player rolls. Your token moves forward that many spaces along the High Land board.

Draw a card

Some spaces trigger an action card draw. The effect applies immediately — good or bad.

Pass the turn

Turn passes in order. Skip effects and card results can change who goes next.

Reach the Sweet Escape

First token to the final space wins. Any overshoot still lands on the finish.
