How to Play

Set up players

Choose 2–10 players, enter names, and pick Local or Online Room mode.

Roll the dice

The active player rolls. Your token moves forward that many spaces along the board.

Land on a card space

Some spaces trigger an action card draw. The effect applies immediately — good or bad.

Pass to next player

Turn passes in order. Skip spaces and card effects can change who goes next.

Reach the Sweet Escape

First token to reach the final space wins. Exact roll not required — any overshoot lands on the finish.
