How it works

Equal odds. Complete cycles. Reproducible sessions.

1. Pick a mode. Strain Picker, Grow Challenge, and Community Wildcard each use their own 18-entry wheel.

2. Complete a cycle. Every unseen entry remains eligible at equal weight. Once chosen, it stays out until all 18 entries have appeared.

3. Share the code. The same mode and code reproduce the same full cycle order. After entry 18, a fresh deterministic cycle begins.
