Observation rule: a visible pattern is evidence, not proof of a single cause. Confirm location, progression, developmental stage, environment, root-zone conditions, pest and pathogen evidence, and recent changes before acting. The Atlas connects what a structure is, what it does, what changes its behavior, and what evidence supports or weakens a plant-health explanation.
