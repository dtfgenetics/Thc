Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Plant science for growers who want to understand their plants

THC is the education system inside DTF Genetics. It covers how cannabis plants work — anatomy, physiology, diagnostics, environment, IPM, and genetics — built around observation, evidence, and correct interpretation before action.

Core rule

A visible symptom is evidence — not proof of a single cause. Observation must be separated from diagnosis and interpreted with plant stage, environment, root-zone data, and recent changes.
