Production standard

Reference image, not decoration.

New education visuals should be photorealistic or academically diagrammed where appropriate, high-resolution raster assets, readable at the size they are actually shown, and specific enough that the image teaches the concept even before the caption is read.

AccurateCorrect anatomy, units, labels, and context.
ReadableLarge labels, controlled density, mobile-safe crops.
Role-specificOne image has one explicit teaching job.
TraceableEvery published asset has subject, caption, alt text, and approval state.
