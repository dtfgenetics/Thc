Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Cultivation Science

Cannabis cultivation science connects plant biology to practical growing decisions. This pathway covers substrate chemistry, irrigation physiology, environmental control, and nutrient management — grounded in plant science rather than rule-of-thumb.

Plant Atlas →DiagnosticsField CardsTools
Approach

Cultivation decisions are most accurate when grounded in plant biology.

Understanding why a plant responds to irrigation timing, VPD, and substrate EC — not just that it does — allows you to troubleshoot correctly and adapt to new situations without needing a rule for every case.

Starting point

Start with the plant's current state, not the plan.

The most common cultivation errors come from following a predetermined schedule rather than reading the plant. Container weight, leaf posture, internode length, root zone measurements, and new growth colour are all real-time feedback signals.
