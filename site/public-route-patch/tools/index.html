Connected workflow

Use the tools in the right order.

A good record improves diagnosis; a good diagnosis points to the right subject literature; the next observation tests whether the interpretation was useful.

1 · Record

Capture plant identity, stage, environment, irrigation, feeding, changes and photos in GrowLens.

2 · Investigate

When a problem appears, move the relevant evidence into Grow Doc and compare plausible causes.

3 · Learn & verify

Open the matching THC subject page, make the smallest defensible correction, then track new growth and progression.
