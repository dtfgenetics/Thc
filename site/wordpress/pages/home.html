Teaching Healthy Cultivation

Learn by goal, not by hunting through pages.

Start with foundations, move into measurement and observation, then use deeper references when the grow actually demands them.

Understand the plant as a system.

Plant biology, light, air, media, temperature, water, nutrients, pest pressure, genetics, and post-harvest outcomes are connected. The learning system should make those connections obvious.
