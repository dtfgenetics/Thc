Visual standard

Images need context, not just resolution.

A polished visual library is trustworthy only when visitors can tell what an image represents, where it came from, and whether it is documentation, illustration, generated concept art, or a general educational reference.

Source

Use original, licensed, public-domain, or properly authorized imagery.

Label

Identify illustration or generated concepts when they could be mistaken for grow documentation.

Context

Do not present generic imagery as a specific DTF plant, cultivar, facility, or product.

Credit

Preserve creator attribution, permission information, and project identity.
