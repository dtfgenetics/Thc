Visual standard

Every image needs a clear identity and purpose.

Visual quality matters, but accurate labeling matters just as much when imagery supports plant science, genetics, or grow documentation.

SourceUse original, licensed, public-domain, or properly authorized imagery.
LabelIdentify illustration or AI-generated concepts when they could be mistaken for real grow documentation.
ContextDo not present stock photography as a DTF plant, cultivar, facility, or product.
CreditPreserve creator credit, submission permission, and project identity.
AccessUse descriptive alternative text and meaningful destinations for visual links.
