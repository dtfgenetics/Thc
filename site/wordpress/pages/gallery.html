Visual standard.

Every public image must be accurate, relevant to its exact placement, readable at the rendered size, and reviewed across phone, tablet, and desktop layouts.

Purpose

Every asset needs a defined job and page destination. No decorative filler.

Quality

Use production-resolution artwork with legible text, clean crops, and no screenshot-like full sheets inside cards.

Context

Do not present generic or educational imagery as a specific DTF cultivar, product, grow, or diagnostic result.

Responsive

Approve the rendered result on mobile, tablet, laptop, and wide desktop before public use.
