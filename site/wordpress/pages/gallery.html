Visual standard.

Every image needs a clear identity, useful context, descriptive alternative text, and the correct destination.

Source

Use original, licensed, public-domain, or properly authorized imagery.

Label

Identify illustration or generated concepts when they could be mistaken for grow documentation.

Context

Do not present generic imagery as a specific DTF plant, cultivar, facility, or product.

Credit

Preserve creator credit, permission, and project identity.
