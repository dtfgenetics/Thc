Information safety

Protect what should stay private.

Verified routing is part of the site structure, not just a footer note. Use published DTF destinations and keep credentials out of community conversations.

01

Never send credentials

Passwords, recovery codes, private keys, and payment-card numbers do not belong in Discord or direct messages.

02

Verify the destination

Checkout and account pages should remain on a DTF-controlled website or the verified service shown by that page.

03

Use published links

Use the Discord invitation published on dtfseeds.com instead of unsolicited links claiming to represent DTF Genetics.
