Before you send anything

Protect your information.

A legitimate support or community conversation does not require passwords, recovery codes, private keys, or payment-card numbers. Use links published on dtfseeds.com and verify where checkout or account actions are taking you.

Never send credentials

Passwords, recovery codes, private keys, and payment-card numbers do not belong in Discord or direct messages.

Verify the destination

Checkout and account pages should remain on a DTF-controlled site or the verified service linked from that page.

Use published links

Prefer links published on dtfseeds.com over unsolicited messages claiming to represent DTF Genetics.

Useful feedback is specific

Bring enough context to reproduce the problem.

For website or game issues, include the page or game name, device, browser, the steps you took, what you expected, and what happened instead. Screenshots help when they do not expose private information.
