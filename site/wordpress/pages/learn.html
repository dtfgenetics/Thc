How THC teaches

Observation before explanation. Verification after action.

Strong lessons should move from evidence to mechanism to decision, without burying the useful answer under unnecessary theory.

01
What is it?

Get the short plain-language answer first.

02
What should I observe?

Know what evidence matters before guessing.

03
What should I measure?

Use the smallest measurement set that can change the decision.

04
What mechanism explains it?

Connect the observation to plant science.

05
What decision follows?

Prefer explicit decision rules over folklore.

06
What can fool me?

Surface confounders, common mistakes, and evidence limits.

07
How do I verify it?

Re-measure or re-observe after the change.

08
Where do I go deeper?

Open the relevant tool, visual, course, or encyclopedia entry.
