How THC teaches

Every strong lesson should answer eight questions.

  1. What is it? Get the short, plain-language answer first.
  2. What should I observe? Know what evidence to look for before guessing.
  3. What should I measure? Use the smallest useful measurement set.
  4. What does the mechanism explain? Connect the observation to plant science.
  5. What decision follows? Use explicit decision rules instead of folklore.
  6. What can fool me? See common mistakes, confounders, and evidence limits.
  7. How do I verify it? Re-measure or re-observe after the change.
  8. Where do I go deeper? Open the linked tool, visual, course, or encyclopedia entry only when needed.

Learning map

Six systems connect most cultivation decisions.

  1. Plant: biology, anatomy, lifecycle, signaling, and plant response.
  2. Environment: temperature, RH, VPD, airflow, light, and CO₂ context.
  3. Root zone: water, media, oxygen, pH, EC, irrigation, and dryback.
  4. Manage: propagation, training, canopy, crop stage, and records.
  5. Protect: sanitation, scouting, differential diagnosis, and IPM.
  6. Finish: harvest, post-harvest, quality, genetics, and iteration.
