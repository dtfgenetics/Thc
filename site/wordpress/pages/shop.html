Store architecture

Products stay separated by what they actually are.

The storefront does not imply that an idea, image, strain project, printable, game or merchandise concept is available unless a real product page exists.

01 · GENETICS

Seed releases

Exact line, generation, seed type, quantity and documented breeding information tied to active listings.

02 · EDUCATION

Printables

Teaching Healthy Cultivation logs, workbooks, diagrams and learning tools only when a finished release is ready.

03 · GAMES

Physical components

Rulebooks, print-and-play releases, expansions and components stay distinct from playable browser builds.

04 · BRAND

Merchandise

Approved apparel, stickers, posters and community items appear only when an actual product route is active.

Before checkout

Verify the listing, not the screenshot.

Use the product page as the live transaction record and the genetics page as the breeding record.

  1. Check the exact product description rather than relying on a social-media image or an older announcement.
  2. Confirm generation, seed type, quantity, availability, shipping eligibility, processing time and return rules before payment.
  3. Use only checkout links hosted on DTF-controlled websites or verified DTF profiles.
  4. Do not send payment in response to an unsolicited direct message.
  5. Use the Discord invitation published on dtfseeds.com rather than unsolicited invitations claiming to represent DTF Genetics.
  6. Follow all local laws and restrictions that apply to the product and destination.
