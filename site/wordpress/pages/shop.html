Before you buy

  • Check the exact product description rather than relying on a social-media image or an older announcement.
  • Confirm generation, seed type, quantity, availability, shipping eligibility, processing time, and return rules before payment.
  • Use only checkout links hosted on DTF-controlled websites or verified DTF profiles.
  • Do not send payment in response to an unsolicited direct message.
  • Use the Discord invitation published on dtfseeds.com rather than unsolicited invitations claiming to represent DTF Genetics.
  • Follow all local laws and restrictions that apply to the product and destination.

Know what you are buying

The store handles the transaction. The genetics catalog explains lineage and breeding direction, Teaching Healthy Cultivation explains the plant science, and the community provides a place for documented grow observations.
