Before you buy

  • Check the exact product description rather than relying on a social-media image.
  • Confirm availability, shipping eligibility, processing time, and return rules before payment.
  • Use only checkout links hosted on DTF-controlled websites or verified DTF profiles.
  • Do not send payment in response to an unsolicited direct message.
  • Use the Discord invitation published on dtfseeds.com rather than unsolicited invitations claiming to represent DTF Genetics.
  • Follow all local laws and restrictions that apply to the product and destination.
