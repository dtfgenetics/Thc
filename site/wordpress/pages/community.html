Community standard

Useful participation needs context.

Respectful discussion, honest documentation, and verified DTF channels make the community more useful for everyone.

01

Document honestly

Separate observation from assumption and label uncertainty instead of presenting guesses as facts.

02

Protect members

Do not pressure people into private transactions, unsafe activity, or sharing information they should keep private.

03

Credit creators

Preserve breeder, artist, photographer, and source attribution when work is discussed or shared.
