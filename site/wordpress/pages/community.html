Community standard.

Useful participation depends on accurate context, respectful discussion, and verified DTF channels. The goal is not to win every argument; it is to leave the shared knowledge better than we found it.

Document honestly

Separate observation from assumption and label uncertainty.

Protect members

Do not pressure people into private transactions or unsafe activity.

Credit creators

Preserve breeder, artist, photographer, and source attribution.
