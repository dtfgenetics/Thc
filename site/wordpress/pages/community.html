Community standard

Useful participation depends on good evidence and good behavior.

These standards protect the quality of discussions, projects, and member interactions.

1Adults only. Follow applicable local laws and platform rules.
2Share observations honestly and label uncertainty instead of presenting guesses as fact.
3Do not pressure members into purchases, trades, private transactions, or unsafe activity.
4Do not present medical, pesticide, electrical, or legal claims as professional advice.
5Critique ideas and methods without attacking people.
6Credit breeders, creators, photographers, artists, and source material appropriately.

Use verified DTF channels

Official invitations, contests, prizes, releases, and payment instructions should be checked against verified DTF Genetics channels. Use the Discord invitation published on this page rather than unsolicited direct-message links claiming to represent DTF Genetics.
