Blue Mango

Lineage: Somango XXL × Blueberry Butcher

Breeding direction: vigorous plants, strong branching, heavy resin production, substantial flower structure, and a candy-blueberry-meets-ripe-mango aromatic profile.

Observed flowering window: commonly described within an 8–10 week planning range under suitable conditions. Phenotype and environment can change actual finish time.

How to read this catalog

  • Lineage identifies documented parents. When lineage is unknown or not yet verified, the page says so instead of filling the gap with a guess.
  • Breeding direction and card traits describe selection goals, observations, or supplied creative copy—not a promise that every seed will express the same traits.
  • Generation and seed type can differ between releases and must be checked on the exact listing.
  • Flowering estimates are planning ranges, not fixed harvest dates.
  • Price and availability can change; a linked WooCommerce product page is the transaction source of truth.
