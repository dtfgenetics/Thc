Catalog standard

Observation over hype.

Lineage identifies documented parentage when a controlled record exists. Breeding direction summarizes selection goals and repeated observations. Flowering windows are planning ranges. Individual plants can vary in structure, aroma, finish time, yield, potency, color, and other traits.
