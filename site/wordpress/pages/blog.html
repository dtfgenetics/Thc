Editorial standard

Useful updates stay connected to evidence and current pages.

The Blog is not a dumping ground for placeholder posts, copied templates, unsupported claims, or stale contact information.

Current

Release status, links, terminology, and site destinations should match the live visitor experience.

Traceable

Scientific claims should connect to the maintained THC evidence system and distinguish observation from established evidence.

Original

DTF updates should describe DTF work directly rather than inheriting generic website-builder copy, stock contact details, or unrelated catalog language.

Looking for something specific?

Use the maintained destination that matches what you need. For site questions or verified contact information, use the current Contact page instead of contact details copied from older templates.
