High IQ — Test Higher Cognition

A cannabis knowledge challenge covering plant science, cultivation, genetics, history, terminology, post-harvest practices, and responsible industry knowledge.

The question system uses Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert levels with one best answer, believable distractors, and a separate explanation-based answer key.

What the status labels mean

  • Browser route: a public DTFSeeds route exists for the game.
  • Development build: core content or play exists, but testing and improvement are still underway.
  • Preproduction: rules, data, artwork, components, or a vertical slice are being prepared before production status.
  • Physical release: a game is not for sale until its individual product page publishes final components, price, policies, and availability.

Help improve the games

Useful playtest reports include the game name, device and browser, player count, exact steps that caused the problem, what was expected, what happened, and a screenshot when possible.
