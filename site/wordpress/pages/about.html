Working standard

Evidence before hype.

DTF should be useful even when the answer is incomplete. That means documenting what happened, separating observation from expectation, and preserving enough context for someone else to understand how a conclusion was reached.

Document what happened

Keep lineage, grow records, project history, measurements, and observations visible.

Separate observation from expectation

Do not turn a breeding direction, one phenotype, or one grow result into a guarantee.

Build for reuse

Shared systems let pages, tools, games, and educational material improve without overwriting one another.
