Working principles

Evidence before hype.

The same standards should show up in genetics, education, software, games, and community work.

1Document what was done and what was observed.
2Do not turn an observation into a guarantee.
3Teach principles, context, and diagnostic thinking.
4Use evidence and clearly label uncertainty.
5Credit original creators and build independent visual identities.
6Test digital products before calling them finished.
