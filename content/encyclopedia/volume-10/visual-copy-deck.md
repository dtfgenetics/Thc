# Part 10 Visual Copy Deck — THC-ENC-181–190

This copy deck keeps future graphics educational, concise, and scientifically qualified.

## 181 — Apical Dominance and Branching Control
Primary caption: Removing or suppressing the dominant shoot changes competitive relationships among axillary buds; the final branch pattern still depends on genotype, developmental stage, and environment.
Labels: shoot apex; axillary bud; node; dominant axis; released branch; recovery checkpoint.

## 182 — Plant Architecture as a Genetic and Managed Trait
Primary caption: Architecture is a phenotype produced by genetics interacting with light, spacing, root conditions, stage, and management.
Labels: internode length; branch angle; branch density; canopy width; apical height; lower-canopy occupancy.

## 183 — Node Development and Training Timing
Primary caption: Tissue age and mechanical condition matter more than a universal calendar week. Observe node development and stem flexibility before intervention.
Labels: expanding node; mature node; flexible internode; lignified tissue; recovery observation.

## 184 — Low-Stress Bending and Thigmomorphogenesis
Primary caption: Mechanical displacement can redistribute branch position without removing the shoot apex, but excessive deformation can become injury.
Labels: elastic bend; persistent bend; crease; split; support point; recovery growth.

## 185 — Topping and Meristem Removal
Primary caption: Topping removes the dominant shoot meristem and redistributes subsequent growth among retained buds; wound recovery and branch response should be recorded.
Labels: removed meristem; retained node; axillary bud; wound zone; new leaders.

## 186 — FIM-Style Cuts and Variable Outcomes
Primary caption: Partial apical cuts do not produce one standardized result. The amount and position of meristematic tissue retained can change regrowth.
Labels: partial cut; retained meristematic tissue; irregular regrowth; outcome variability.

## 187 — Mainlining and Repeated Structural Training
Primary caption: Repeated topping, branch selection, and repositioning accumulate interventions over time. Uniformity must be evaluated against recovery cost and genotype response.
Labels: first division; selected arms; repeated cut; support; recovery interval; final canopy plane.

## 188 — Screen and Trellis Systems
Primary caption: A trellis is a spatial support and distribution system. Evaluate branch support, canopy occupancy, measurement access, light distribution, and airflow—not just flatness.
Labels: support plane; branch position; PPFD point; airflow path; service access; overcrowded zone.

## 189 — Pruning Lower Shoots and Sink Competition
Primary caption: Shaded lower growth can remain a competing sink, but removal also changes whole-plant structure. Compare canopy position and crop response rather than assuming all lower shoots are waste.
Labels: upper source leaves; developing sink; shaded lower shoot; removed shoot; redistributed branch growth.

## 190 — Defoliation: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Risk
Primary caption: Defoliation trades leaf area for altered internal light and airflow. The useful question is whether the plant and canopy recover in a way that improves the measured crop outcome.
Labels: leaf area removed; internal PPFD; airflow; source capacity; recovery; comparison zone.
