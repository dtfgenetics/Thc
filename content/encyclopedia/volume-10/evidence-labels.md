# Evidence Labels for Part 10

Use these labels in future lesson revisions and visuals:

- **Cannabis-specific evidence** — direct study of Cannabis sativa under stated conditions.
- **Horticultural mechanism** — broader plant-science evidence used to explain a plausible mechanism.
- **System-dependent result** — outcome depends materially on cultivar, density, timing, environment, or treatment intensity.
- **Measurement recommendation** — a record or comparison method used to evaluate the local response; not a universal biological target.
- **Evidence gap** — a widely discussed practice for which standardized cannabis trials remain insufficient for a universal claim.
