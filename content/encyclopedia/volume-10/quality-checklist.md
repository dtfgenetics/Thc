# Part 10 Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before each Canopy Architecture and Training Biology lesson is moved into the production manifest.

- Controlled lesson ID and title match Master Content Map v1.1.
- Objective teaches a biological or measurement concept, not just a grower action.
- Direct cannabis evidence and general plant-physiology evidence are labeled distinctly.
- No fixed week, node count, leaf-removal percentage, or guaranteed yield claim is presented as universal.
- Genotype, plant density, developmental stage, environment, and treatment intensity are treated as material context.
- At least one before/after measurement or repeatable observation is specified.
- Recovery and failure modes are included where tissue is bent, cut, removed, or restrained.
- Misconceptions are explicit and useful to growers.
- Evidence limits are visible in the lesson itself.
- Cross-links connect physiology, lighting, diagnostics, and adjacent canopy lessons.
- Teaching visual is mechanism-first and linked to the visual plan.
- Publication authorization and independent-review status are explicit.
