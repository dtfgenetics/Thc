# Part 10 Learning Path

Recommended order for Training & Canopy Architecture:

### Foundation
1. THC-ENC-181 — Apical Dominance and Branching Control
2. THC-ENC-182 — Plant Architecture as a Genetic and Managed Trait
3. THC-ENC-183 — Node Development and Training Timing

### Mechanical and Meristem Interventions
4. THC-ENC-184 — Low-Stress Bending and Thigmomorphogenesis
5. THC-ENC-185 — Topping and Meristem Removal
6. THC-ENC-186 — FIM-Style Cuts and Variable Outcomes
7. THC-ENC-187 — Mainlining and Repeated Structural Training

### Canopy Distribution and Removal Decisions
8. THC-ENC-188 — Screen and Trellis Systems
9. THC-ENC-189 — Pruning Lower Shoots and Sink Competition
10. THC-ENC-190 — Defoliation: Mechanisms, Evidence, and Risk

Before applying any technique, learners should be able to state the canopy problem, identify the tissue being changed, define the measurement that will show whether the change helped, and identify the major confounders.
