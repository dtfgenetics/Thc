# Part 10 Batch 02 Release Notes

Batch: `volume10-batch02`
Lessons: `THC-ENC-186` through `THC-ENC-190`
Topic: Training & Canopy Architecture

## Release controls

This batch is owner-authorized for website publication with independent specialist review still pending. Publication does not represent independent approval.

The release-time review specifically preserves these limits:

- FIM-style cuts have variable outcomes and are not represented as a standardized meristem technique.
- Mainlining is treated as repeated structural training, not a universally superior architecture.
- Screen and trellis systems are evaluated by support, spatial distribution, light sampling, and access rather than by appearance alone.
- Lower-shoot pruning is framed through source-sink competition and canopy position without promising a universal yield increase.
- Defoliation is framed as a tradeoff between leaf-area removal, internal light, airflow, source capacity, and recovery. No fixed percentage or flowering-week schedule is authorized.
