# Part 10 — Canopy Architecture and Training Biology

Controlled lesson range: **THC-ENC-181–200**

This part explains how spacing, pruning, bending, trellising, leaf-area decisions, support systems, and recovery change plant architecture, light distribution, airflow, and source–sink relationships.

## Evidence rules

- Do not convert cultivar-specific results into universal cannabis rules.
- Distinguish direct cannabis trials from general plant-physiology mechanisms.
- Do not prescribe fixed training weeks, node counts, removal percentages, or guaranteed yield responses unless a controlled source specifically supports the exact context.
- Record the treatment, plant stage, genotype, canopy density, recovery, environmental context, and final crop response.
- Treat training as a measured canopy intervention, not a ritualized schedule.
