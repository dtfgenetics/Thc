# Part 10 Visitor Expectations

A visitor opening any Training & Canopy Architecture lesson should be able to answer:

- What plant structure or physiological relationship is this lesson about?
- What does the intervention change biologically?
- What outcome is plausible, and what outcome is not guaranteed?
- What should be measured before and after?
- What signs indicate poor recovery or excessive intervention?
- Which claims come from direct cannabis evidence, and which are broader plant-physiology context?
- Which adjacent lessons explain the prerequisite science?

The topic hub should favor these decisions over technique hype. Named training methods are secondary to plant structure, measurement, recovery, and evidence quality.
