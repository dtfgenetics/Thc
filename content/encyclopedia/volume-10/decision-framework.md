# Canopy Intervention Decision Framework

1. **Observe** — identify the actual architectural limitation.
2. **Measure** — record stage, genotype, canopy dimensions, branch distribution, light distribution, and visible stress.
3. **Select** — choose an intervention that directly addresses the limitation with the least unnecessary tissue disruption.
4. **Document** — record exactly what was bent, cut, removed, tied, or repositioned.
5. **Recover** — verify wound condition, leaf posture, new growth, and return to stable growth before stacking additional stress.
6. **Re-measure** — repeat the same canopy and light observations from the same positions.
7. **Compare** — use an untreated plant, zone, prior cycle, or clearly defined baseline when possible.
8. **Decide** — continue, modify, or stop based on measured response rather than technique tradition.
