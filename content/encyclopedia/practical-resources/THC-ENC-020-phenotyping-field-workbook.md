# THC-ENC-020 | Repeatable Plant Phenotyping Field Workbook

**Teaching Healthy Cultivation — practical resource (draft for editorial and accessibility review)**  
Companion lesson: [Morphological Measurement and Botanical Recordkeeping](/learn/encyclopedia/thc-enc-020/)  
Status: instructional worksheet; not an independently validated experimental protocol.

## Learning outcomes

After completing this exercise, the learner should be able to (1) identify a sampling unit, (2) write operational measurement definitions, (3) separate raw observations from inferences, and (4) flag uncertainty or missing data rather than inventing a result.

## Protocol: one observer, two repeat measurements

1. Assign each plant an anonymous, stable **plant ID**; never use only the cultivar name. Record date, local time and time zone, plant stage *with your stage definition*, growing medium, and measurement observer.
2. **Height (cm):** with a ruler or measuring tape, measure vertically from the defined substrate surface at the stem base to the highest natural plant point, without straightening or stretching the plant. Record whether stakes or training changed the natural position.
3. **Canopy span (cm):** measure the widest horizontal extent in a defined compass or room orientation (axis A), then the perpendicular horizontal span (axis B). Do not substitute pot width. State if foliage was moved.
4. **Internode (mm):** label two adjacent, unambiguous stem nodes on the *same branch*; record branch and node indices, then measure the straight-line distance between their attachment landmarks. Do not compare an unlabelled internode on one plant with a different position on another.
5. Record every measurement twice without copying the first reading. Record the instrument's smallest graduation; do **not** equate its graduation with true accuracy.
6. Preserve original images and files. For photographs record plant ID, image filename, date/time, camera, position/view, lighting, and a scale reference in the plane of the feature being measured.
7. Mark measurements as **missing**, **not applicable**, or **not measurable** with a reason; do not enter zero for missing observations.
8. If two readings disagree, keep both and flag the difference for review rather than silently deleting one.

## Example: report uncertainty rather than manufacture precision

*Illustrative numbers, not findings from a real cultivar or experiment.* Plant ID P-014, same observer, same measurement session:

| Variable | Reading 1 | Reading 2 | Mean | Absolute repeat difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Height (cm) | 42.0 | 42.5 | 42.25 | 0.5 cm |
| Canopy axis A (cm) | 35.0 | 35.4 | 35.2 | 0.4 cm |

The mean is an arithmetic summary, **not a claim of accuracy to 0.01 cm**. If the tape graduation or positioning error is coarser than the displayed digits, round the reported result appropriately and keep unrounded readings in the raw record. Two measurements cannot establish between-observer repeatability, instrument calibration, or statistical confidence intervals.

## Interpretation and quality-control questions

1. What exactly is the sampling unit: plant, branch, organ, photograph, or a group of plants?
2. Could irrigation, time of day, training, plant water status, or the observer have changed the measurement?
3. Were the same landmarks and developmental stages used for every plant and every observation date?
4. What observations support the conclusion, and what would require a control group or replication?
5. If an image looks larger or more resinous, what would be needed before claiming higher yield or cannabinoid content?

**Model interpretation:** “Plant P-014 had two height readings of 42.0 and 42.5 cm under the stated measurement protocol.” Do not write “this genotype grows taller because of treatment X” without controlled comparisons and appropriate replication.

## Record quality checklist

- [ ] Stable IDs and timestamps, including time zone
- [ ] Sampling unit and developmental stage defined
- [ ] Units, instrument, measurement landmarks, and observer recorded
- [ ] Raw readings preserved; missing values distinguished from zero
- [ ] Images linked by identifiers with scale and provenance
- [ ] Treatment and environment context recorded without implying causation
- [ ] Interpretations kept separate from raw observations
- [ ] Conflicting readings and limitations documented
