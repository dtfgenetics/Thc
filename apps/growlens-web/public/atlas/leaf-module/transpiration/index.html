Transpiration

Transpiration is the movement of water from the root zone through the plant and out through leaves. It drives cooling, transport, and plant posture.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext

Pathway

Water enters roots, moves through xylem, reaches leaves, evaporates from internal leaf surfaces, and exits through stomata as vapor.

Why growers track it

  • It affects nutrient transport.
  • It changes leaf temperature.
  • It influences turgor and wilting.
  • It connects irrigation to VPD and airflow.

Stress patterns

Too much demand can cause tacoing, edge curl, wilt, crisp margins, or stalled growth. Too little demand can slow movement and increase disease pressure.
