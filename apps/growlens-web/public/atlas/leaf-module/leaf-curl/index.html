Leaf Curl

Leaf curl describes posture: tacoing, canoeing, clawing, drooping, twisting, or cupping. Shape points to what to check next.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext: Nutrient Symptoms

Visual reference slot

Needed asset: Leaf Curl Pattern Guide comparing taco, canoe, claw, droop, twist, cup, and wilt with likely context checks.

Field checklist

  • Name the posture instead of saying “curl.”
  • Record canopy zone, time of day, and speed of onset.
  • Check whether stems are rigid, soft, brittle, or normal.

Measurements to pair

  • VPD, temperature, RH, airflow, PPFD.
  • Substrate moisture, irrigation timing, EC/pH.
  • Fan direction and recent environment swings.

Common mistakes

  • Treating curl as a nutrient issue first.
  • Ignoring heat/light and water balance.
  • Confusing droop from dryness with droop from saturation.

Grow Doc prompt

Classify leaf posture precisely, then compare canopy position, VPD, PPFD, temperature, airflow, substrate moisture, watering timing, EC/pH, and recent changes.
