Core rule

Observation first. Diagnosis second.

A leaf symptom is evidence, not proof. Yellowing, browning, spots, curling, wilting, burnt tips, or pest marks must be interpreted with plant stage, symptom location, environment, root-zone readings, recent changes, and direct inspection.

First field card

Leaf Diagnostic Field Card

The first printable tool should guide growers through location, pattern, measurements, underside inspection, documentation photos, and next-step triage before making corrections.

Measurements

Data to collect with leaf symptoms

  • Old growth or new growth
  • Upper canopy or lower canopy
  • Whole plant, one branch, one leaf, or multiple plants
  • PPFD / DLI / light distance
  • Temperature, RH, VPD, airflow
  • pH, EC, substrate moisture, watering and feed history
  • Pest inspection: top, underside, nodes, growing tips, media surface
  • Recent changes and progression photos
