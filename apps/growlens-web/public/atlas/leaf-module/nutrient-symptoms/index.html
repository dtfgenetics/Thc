Nutrient Symptoms on Leaves

Nutrient-like symptoms must be interpreted with pH, EC, feed history, roots, watering, stage, and environment. Visuals alone are not proof.

Visual reference slot

Needed asset: Nutrient Symptom Triage Card organizing old/new growth, interveinal patterns, margins, tips, spots, and root-zone checks.

Field checklist

  • Separate old-growth symptoms from new-growth symptoms.
  • Record mobile-vs-immobile style pattern without forcing a diagnosis.
  • Check whether pH/EC or watering explains availability.

Measurements to pair

  • pH, EC/PPM, feed schedule, source water.
  • Media type, irrigation volume, runoff/dryback.
  • Temperature, RH, VPD, and root health.

Common mistakes

  • Adding supplements before measuring basics.
  • Ignoring root stress or lockout-like conditions.
  • Confusing pest damage and environmental stress for nutrients.

Grow Doc prompt

Evaluate nutrient-like leaf symptoms with old/new growth, symptom pattern, pH, EC, feed history, irrigation, media, roots, environment, pests, and recent changes.
