Chlorosis

Chlorosis means yellowing from reduced chlorophyll. It is a symptom category, not a diagnosis. The pattern and location decide what to check next.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext: Necrosis

Pattern language

  • Uniform yellowing across old leaves.
  • Interveinal yellowing with green veins.
  • New-growth yellowing.
  • Patchy yellowing near pests, roots, or damaged tissue.

What to check

Record plant stage, canopy location, pH, EC, irrigation pattern, root health, light intensity, VPD, recent feed changes, and pest evidence before treating.

Common look-alikes

Normal senescence, nitrogen limitation, magnesium issues, iron availability, root stress, pH-driven availability, overwatering, pest damage, and early disease can all produce yellowing.
