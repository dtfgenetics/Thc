Stomata

Stomata are microscopic leaf pores that balance carbon dioxide entry with water vapor loss. They connect light, VPD, temperature, humidity, water status, and growth.

PreviousLeaf ModuleNext

Function

Open stomata allow CO2 to enter for photosynthesis, but they also allow water vapor to leave. Closed stomata conserve water but can slow photosynthesis and growth.

What affects them

  • VPD and leaf temperature.
  • Root-zone moisture and oxygen.
  • Light intensity and photoperiod.
  • Plant stress, disease pressure, and chemical exposure.

Diagnostic use

Leaf tacoing, edge curl, wilting, or sudden droop may reflect stomatal and water-status stress, but the visual symptom must be checked against environment and root-zone conditions.
