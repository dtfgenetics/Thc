Root System Module

Water Uptake

Water uptake links substrate moisture, root health, transpiration demand, leaf posture, and nutrient movement. Problems can show as droop, curl, slow growth, wilt, or deficiency-like symptoms.

DrybackTranspiration

What to learn

  • Roots absorb water from the media solution around them.
  • Moisture must be available without eliminating oxygen.
  • High transpiration demand can outpace uptake.
  • Low oxygen or damaged roots can reduce uptake even when media is wet.
  • Leaf wilt can come from too dry, too wet, heat, root damage, or high VPD.

Visual reference slot

High-quality visual needed:

Root-to-leaf water pathway showing media water, root absorption, xylem movement, transpiration, and leaf posture response.

Measurements to pair

Container weight, substrate moisture, irrigation timing, VPD, leaf temperature, root-zone temperature, pH, EC, and runoff trend.
