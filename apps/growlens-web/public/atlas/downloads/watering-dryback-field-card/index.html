Watering & Dryback Field Card

Use this card to evaluate wet-to-dry behavior, container weight, substrate moisture, irrigation timing, and leaf posture before changing feed or watering volume.

Print / Save PDFOpen Dryback Lesson

Field checklist

  • Lift or compare container weight before watering.
  • Check whether the top, middle, and lower root zone are behaving differently.
  • Record last irrigation time, volume, runoff, and current plant posture.
  • Separate wilt from heat/VPD stress and root-zone saturation.
  • Watch whether symptoms improve, worsen, or repeat after watering.
Rule: watering is a root-zone decision, not a fixed calendar habit.
