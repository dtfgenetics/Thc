Chlorosis Pattern Checklist

Use this checklist when leaves are yellowing. The goal is to describe the yellowing pattern accurately before deciding whether the issue is nutrient, root-zone, pest, disease, age, or environment related.

Chlorosis pattern comparison chart

Final visual should compare uniform yellowing, interveinal chlorosis, marginal yellowing, old-growth-first yellowing, and new-growth-first yellowing at the same scale.

Field checklist

  • Does yellowing start on old growth or new growth?
  • Do veins stay green while tissue between veins yellows?
  • Is yellowing uniform, marginal, patchy, or spreading from tips?
  • Do pH, EC, moisture, and recent feed history support a nutrient explanation?
  • Could light, temperature, VPD, roots, pests, or natural senescence explain it?
Rule: chlorosis is a symptom category, not a diagnosis.
